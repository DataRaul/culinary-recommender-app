## Material contributor / provenance ambiguity

The exact 1861 text's own preface expressly states that the **matter of the recipes** was supplied in part by:

- many correspondents of the *Englishwoman's Domestic Magazine*, including formulas for original preparations;
- a large private circle;
- friends in England, Scotland, Ireland, France and Germany;
- study of works of the best modern cookery writers.

The book therefore does not present every recipe's underlying matter as solely authored by Isabella Beeton.

The same preface separately identifies other chapters as contributed by an experienced surgeon and a solicitor. Those non-recipe chapters are not the basis of this hold, but they independently confirm that the volume is a multi-contributor compilation.

The pinned ORA representation assigns the entire candidate recipe cohort the single record-level author value **"Isabella Beeton"**. It does not preserve recipe-by-recipe contributor identities or source-lineage distinctions sufficient to establish which of the 1,382 extracted recipes are Beeton-originated, correspondent-originated, privately supplied, or derived from other cookery writers.

That is a material provenance boundary for this exact reusable representation, not a merely bibliographic issue.
