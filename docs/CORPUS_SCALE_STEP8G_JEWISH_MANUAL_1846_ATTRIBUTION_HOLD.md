## Exact work / edition identity

The 1846 title page identifies *The Jewish Manual; or Practical Information in Jewish and Modern Cookery* and credits only **"Edited by a Lady."**

Project Gutenberg preserves the same anonymous title-page credit. The National Library of Israel describes the original 1846 work in the same way.

The book is commonly attributed in later cataloguing and scholarship to Lady Judith Cohen Montefiore, which is why the ORA metadata already marks the name as `(attr.)`.

## Material authorship ambiguity

The National Library of Israel's source-specific discussion states that although the book has since been attributed to Lady Judith Montefiore, there is **no documentation or concrete proof** supporting that hypothesis.

Other catalogues characterize Montefiore as probable or bracketed attribution rather than an identity stated by the original edition.

Therefore the exact 1846 source does not establish the natural-person author/editor identity used by ORA with documentary certainty.
