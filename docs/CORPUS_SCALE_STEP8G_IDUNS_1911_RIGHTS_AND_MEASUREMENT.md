1. Project Runeberg identifies *Iduns kokbok* as a work by **Elisabeth Östman**, published in Stockholm in **1911**, and states that its digital facsimile is the **first edition from 1911**. The title page identifies Elisabeth Östman, *Iduns kokbok*, Stockholm 1911, Aktiebolaget Ljus.

2. The Internet Archive item used by the ORA source URL identifies the item as *Iduns kokbok*, publication date **1911**, contributed from the Project Runeberg/Arkivkopia facsimile lineage. The cohort is therefore tied to the exact historical first-edition work, not a later revision.

3. The Swedish Women's Biographical Dictionary records **Laura Elisabeth Östman, 1869-08-03 — 1933-03-03**, identifies her as the author of *Iduns kokbok*, and records her death in 1933. Project Runeberg independently lists Elisabeth Östman as **1869–1933** and lists the 1911 first edition separately from later editions.

4. For Spain-facing term analysis, the current Spanish Intellectual Property Act transitional provision 4 preserves the duration under the 10 January 1879 Act for authors who died before 7 December 1987. Article 6 of the 1879 Act provided authorial exploitation rights for life plus **80 years**. On the documented 1933 death year, that exploitation term expired long before 2026.

5. Project Runeberg states that its publication model is based on works whose authors/creative contributors have been dead for more than the applicable 70-year term. This provides independent source-layer corroboration but is not used as a substitute for the Spain-facing term calculation above.

6. The source identity is edition-specific. Later editions of *Iduns kokbok* exist and are not silently substituted into this cohort. No later revisions, modern introductions, annotations, images, transcriber-created editorial material, or later-edition changes are imported as source authority.
