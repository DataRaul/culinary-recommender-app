`PASS_RIGHTS_VERIFIED_BOUNDED_ATRUTEL_1874_FIRST_EDITION_AUTHOR_TERM_EXPIRED`

This is an operational source-admission classification for this exact cohort, not a general legal opinion, and it does not rely on ORA's licence marker alone.

1. The exact 1874 scan title page identifies the work as *An Easy and Economical Book of Jewish Cookery, upon Strictly Orthodox Principles*, by **Mrs. J. Atrutel**, printed in London by **Alabaster & Passmore**, 31 Little Britain, in **1874**. The title page's historical "All Rights Reserved" notice is retained as provenance rather than treated as evidence of a surviving 2026 term.

2. The exact book's preface is signed **Estella Atrutel** and describes the work as the product of her own experience and arrangement. Wellcome Collection catalogs the same 1874 work under **Atrutel, Estella** and marks the item **Public Domain Mark**.

3. Independent scholarship identifies Mrs. J. Atrutel as **Estella Benzaquen**, who married Judah Atrutel in 1863, and describes her prior professional catering experience. A Sephardic cemetery record identifies **Estella Atrutel née Benzaquen**, wife of Judah, and records her death in **1884**.

4. Spain's current Copyright Act, transitional provision 4, preserves the duration provided by the 10 January 1879 Act for authors who died before 7 December 1987. Article 6 of the 1879 Act provided the author's rights for life and an eighty-year post-mortem term. On the documented 1884 death year, Atrutel's authorial exploitation term is long expired by 2026.

5. The source identity is edition-specific. Later editions exist, including an 1894 edition, but this cohort is tied only to the exact **1874 first edition** source. No later editorial additions, modern introductions, annotations, images, or transcriber-created material are imported as source authority.
