## Spain-facing term problem

Spain's current Copyright Act provides, in transitional provision 4, that works created by authors who died before 7 December 1987 retain the duration provided by the 10 January 1879 Intellectual Property Act. Article 6 of the 1879 Act provides an eighty-year post-mortem term for the author's rights.

The source-specific review located multiple independent bibliographic records for Florence Kreisler Greenbaum and the 1918 work, but the reliable records reviewed do **not** establish a verified birth/death date for Greenbaum.

Examples include:

- Project Gutenberg's author/work record;
- Open Library's exact 1918 edition and author record;
- New York Academy of Medicine catalog record;
- New York Public Library bibliography of Jewish cookery.

Those records identify the author but do not provide a verified death date sufficient to compute the Spain-facing term.

The work itself calls Greenbaum the **author and compiler**. Its historical copyright notice is not a substitute for establishing the applicable Spain-facing author term and chain of rights.
