The V0 corpus is deliberately small and quality-oriented. Corpus expansion is evidence-driven rather than a recipe-count race.

Fridge search is intentionally a deterministic retrieval/ranking layer over the same recipe corpus and safety boundaries, not a separate generative recipe engine.
