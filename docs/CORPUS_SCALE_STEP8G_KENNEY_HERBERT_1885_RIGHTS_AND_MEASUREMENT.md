3. Open Library independently maps Internet Archive identifier `culinaryjottings00kenn` to its 1885 **5th edition**, by A. R. Kenney-Herbert, published by Higginbotham and Co. in Madras. A modern Prospect Books facsimile description likewise identifies the reused historical base as the 1885 fifth edition and notes the work was first published in 1878.

4. Spain's current Copyright Act transitional provision 4 states that works by authors who died before 7 December 1987 retain the duration provided by the 10 January 1879 Act. Article 6 of the 1879 Act provided the author's rights for life and an eighty-year post-mortem term. Kenney-Herbert died in 1916, so the exploitation term is long expired by 2026.

5. The project preserves author, pseudonym, work, exact edition, year, publisher and source provenance. Any later facsimile introduction, editorial layer, translation, illustration or third-party addition requires separate review and is not imported from a modern reprint.
